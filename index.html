What is this dashboard?

What it isThe results of my Google Forms survey (43 responses, 9 questions) turned into charts you can explore.
Who it is forMe and design reviewers, to decide which features the Rivaayat State Atlas needs first.
How to use itTap a ring to open that question. Switch charts, sort, compare, hover for details, flip the cards at the bottom.

Survey results show what people say, not what they do. 43 responses are not statistically representative, and ages were not asked.

Design brief

UserMe and design reviewersDesign-literate, moderate data skills. They decide which Atlas features to build first.
Use caseDecide with evidenceUsed at a desk or in a critique to check which survey result backs each design choice.
Dashboard typeAnalyticalIt supports exploring and comparing answers, not live monitoring.
Information hierarchyHeadlines → questions → insightsKey numbers first, details next, design actions last.
Scanning patternF-patternTitle and key numbers run across the top; question chips and charts run down the left.
